01 / Lopez Island

Built for salt air
and open water.

Lopez is flatter and more open than its neighbors, which means less shade but far more sustained wind across farmland and shoreline alike.

Where we work

Lopez Island sits the southernmost of the main San Juans, inside our service area. Wind rating and fastening schedule drive the spec here. We stage materials by ferry and keep crews on island until the work is closed in.

What we see most

Open, sustained wind exposure with little natural windbreak.

How we start

With a free assessment — inside and out — followed by a written scope and estimate within 24 hours. No pressure, no obligation.
